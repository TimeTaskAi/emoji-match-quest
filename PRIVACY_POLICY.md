---

## 2. Our Core Privacy Principles
1. **No Personal Account Required:** You do not need to register an account, sign in with social media, or provide personal details.
2. **Offline-First Playability:** The game is fully playable offline. Progress is stored locally on your device.
3. **No Sensitive Data Collection:** We never collect sensitive personal information such as biometric data, location (GPS), financial payment credentials, contacts, microphone, or camera data.
4. **Child & Family Friendly:** We comply with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy.

---

## 3. Information We Collect and Process
- **Locally Stored Data:** Level progress, high scores, coins, gems, and settings.
- **Anonymous Telemetry & Leaderboard:** Random anonymous device ID, anonymous player tag, high scores, operating system version.
- **Advertising (Google AdMob):** Google Advertising ID (AAID), coarse IP location, ad interactions, diagnostic metrics.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob:** Serving in-game advertisements.
- **Google Play Services:** Core platform services.
- **Supabase:** Anonymous cloud database for global leaderboards and remote config.
